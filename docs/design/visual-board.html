01 · Direction

Quiet luxury, editorial calm

The product is a paid membership to a person, not a content wall. The interface is therefore dark, slow and typographic: deep plum surfaces, one warm metal accent, generous spacing, and photography from the creator doing the colour work. Nothing glows, nothing bounces, no gradients-on-everything.

What it says

A private room, not a marketplace. The subscriber should feel they are reading a letter from someone, not browsing a store.

What it refuses

Neon, "creator economy" gradients, emoji-heavy buttons, badges competing for attention, and any red/orange that reads as adult-industry cliché. The accent is amber, closer to brass than to fire.

How it scales

Every screen is built from nine tokens (colour, type, radius, spacing, elevation). The same tokens drive Compose and the PWA, so a creator page cannot drift between app and web.

Rules

Creator names, prices in headlines and section titles use Fraunces (OFL). Interface text uses Inter (OFL); both licences are compatible with the project's no-copyleft rule.

Numerals are tabular wherever money appears, so balances do not jitter when they animate.

Minimum body size 15sp; nothing below 12sp exists in the UI. Line height 1.5 for prose, 1.15 for display.

Dynamic Type: the layout reflows to 200% without truncating a price or a label.

04 · Iconography

2px strokes, rounded caps, one geometry

24dp grid, 2dp stroke, 4dp corner radius, optical alignment rather than mathematical centring. No filled icons except the active navigation state, so "where am I" reads instantly.

08 · Motion, feedback, accessibility

Motion

Press 120ms, sheets 200ms ease-out, screen transitions 240ms shared-axis. No bounce, no spring on money. Respect reduce-motion by replacing transitions with 80ms fades.

Feedback

Success is a mint inline confirmation, never a modal. Failure states name the next action ("Card declined — try another method"), and destructive actions require typing the word, not a second tap.

Accessibility

4.5:1 minimum body contrast (measured above), 48dp targets, TalkBack labels on every icon, full Dynamic Type to 200%, dark-only for v1 with a light theme deferred to P2.

Policy surfaces

The public profile never shows nudity or suggestion; locked thumbnails are blurred and labelled. The 18+ gate is part of sign-up and cannot be dismissed without leaving. Paid surfaces stay non-explicit by policy.

Trust

Payout amounts, hold periods and commission are shown before the creator commits. KYC state is always visible. No dark patterns on cancellation: the button is a normal secondary button, not hidden.

What is deliberately absent

No light mode, no accent colour per creator, no sticker/emoji layer, no infinite-scroll autoplay. Each of those weakens the "private room" reading and adds accessibility surface we cannot yet test.
