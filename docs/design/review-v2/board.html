The journey

Five destinations in the tab bar. Everything else is a pushed screen or a sheet.

Navigation model

Home (discovery) · Subs (creators you pay for) · Create (creator studio, hidden until the account publishes) · Alerts · You (profile, billing, settings). Checkout, media and age verification are full-screen pushes with a single back path; unlock, filters and confirmations are bottom sheets.

Direction notes

What this direction is

Dark, warm and quiet. Serif display against a neutral UI face, one accent (amber) that only marks money and verification, mint for success, red reserved for failure. No gradients on text, no glow, no drop shadows on cards — separation comes from 1 px hairlines and background steps. Money is always monospaced and tabular so columns line up.

Light theme: deliberately deferred. The product is consumed at night, and the media viewer must not flash white. If a light theme is wanted, it should be a second pass over the same tokens.

Content rules visible in the UI: the public surface never implies nudity, and locked surfaces say what they are instead of teasing an explicit image.
