Direction 03 · dark theme + motion
26 November 2026 · companion to board.png
18 dark screens · 6 motion patterns

The same room,
at night.

Dark is not the light theme with the values flipped. The ground keeps a brown undertone so paper stock becomes a dim room rather than a void, ink is warm bone instead of white, and the oxblood splits in two: a deeper fill for actions, a lifted tone for type. Nothing turns blue, nothing turns grey, and the eclipse mark still reads at 16 px.

Light remains the default experience. Dark is offered for night use and chosen per device — system, light, or dark — with the choice stored before the first frame so no launch flashes paper.

Design rules

Ground #12100F — warm, not black. Ink #F3EBE1 — bone, not white.

Oxblood keeps its hue and gains a second tone for text, because 4.5:1 at 11 px is not negotiable.

Surfaces separate by value, not by shadow: ground → card → raised.

Status stays sage / brass / red, lifted for legibility.

Contrast tested in VesperPaletteTest, not eyeballed.

03Motionthree durations, one curve, one switch
Feedback · 140 ms
FastOutSlowIn · press, select, chip

Buttons and rows answer the thumb

Press scales to 0.97 and the tab tint crossfades. Under 150 ms so it reads as the control, not as an animation.

Transition · 220 ms
FastOutSlowIn · destinations, sheets, overlays

Screens move 1/12 of the width

Destinations slide with the tab order and fade; creator profile rises 1/10 of its height; checkout sheet rises from the bottom over a fading scrim.

Reveal · 320 ms
FastOutSlowIn · first composition only

Content arrives once, then stays

Cards fade 0.35 → 1 and lift 8 dp, staggered 40 ms per item to a 240 ms cap so a long feed never waits.

What animates, and why

Destination change — direction is information: the slide tells you where you went.
Creator profile — a pushed surface, so it rises over the feed it came from.
Checkout sheet — scrim fades, sheet rises; closing returns to the tier list unchanged.
Content reveal — one per screen, because a feed that appears all at once feels frozen.
Unlock and payment confirmation — the result card slides in on the same layer as the sheet it replaces.
Buttons, tabs, theme rows — 140 ms press scale and colour crossfade only.

What stays still, and why

Media, prices, contract text — moving money or legal copy is a distraction and a liability.
Scrolling — no parallax, no sticky transforms, no image zoom on scroll.
Loading — skeletons match geometry and hold still; no shimmer loop to drain battery.
Error and declined states — a shake would mock the user at the worst moment.
Reduced motion — every duration resolves to 0 when Android's animator or transition scale is 0; layout, ripple and navigation are unchanged, so nothing becomes unreachable.
